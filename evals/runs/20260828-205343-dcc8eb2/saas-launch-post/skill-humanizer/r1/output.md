## What's inside

Orbit ships with the three things we spent the most time on:

- Speed. Approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes.
- Visibility. Every handoff lives on one board: who owns it, what's blocking it, when it's due.
- Control. Admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them from then on.

Those features connect across the stack instead of sitting in separate tabs, and the whole thing stays lightweight without leaving gaps. It runs on the same values as the rest of Fluxframe: excellence, collaboration, innovation.

The workflow itself is short. Request → review → approve → done. It took our roadmap three years to move Fluxframe from weakness to strength to dominance in the automation market, and Orbit is the fast, reliable, easy part of that story.

The result is fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox, and requests that stop going missing. It's worth asking why approvals stall in the first place, and how many hours your managers would get back if they didn't.

Orbit's dashboard is the single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what and when, and the audit log gives compliance teams proof that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
