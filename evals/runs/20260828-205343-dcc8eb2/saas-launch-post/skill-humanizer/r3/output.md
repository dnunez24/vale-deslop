## What's inside

Orbit ships with three pillars our team obsessed over:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** every handoff (who owns it, what's blocking it, when it's due) lives on one board
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them automatically

The platform integrates features across the stack, and we kept it light without leaving out what teams actually need. Excellence, collaboration, and innovation are the things we built it on.

The workflow is straightforward: request → review → approve → done. Orbit is fast, it's reliable, and people pick it up without much training. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from weakness to strength to dominance in the automation market.
