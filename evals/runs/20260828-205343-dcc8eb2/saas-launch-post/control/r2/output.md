## What's Inside

Orbit ships with three things our team obsessed over:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** every handoff — who owns it, what's blocking it, when it's due — lives on one board
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them automatically

The workflow itself is simple: request → review → approve → done. It took Fluxframe three years to move from a weak position in the automation market, to a strong one, to the leading one, and the platform holds up on speed, reliability, and ease of use.

The upshot is fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox — and a clearer answer to why approvals stall in the first place.

Orbit's dashboard is a single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed is a running record of who touched what and when. The audit log gives compliance teams proof that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
