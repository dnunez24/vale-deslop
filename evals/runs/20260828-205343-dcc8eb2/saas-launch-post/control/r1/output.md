Most teams underestimate how much time approvals eat every week. So instead of bolting automation onto our old dashboard, we rebuilt the approval engine from scratch, piece by piece. Stalled approvals, we've come to believe, are the single biggest drag on a growing team's velocity.

## What's Inside

Orbit ships with three pillars our team obsessed over:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** every handoff — who owns it, what's blocking it, when it's due — lives on one board
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them automatically

The pieces are built to work together rather than sit side by side as separate modules, so requests, approvals, and reporting all live in the same place instead of being stitched together through separate integrations.

The workflow itself is straightforward: request → review → approve → done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap has shifted increasingly toward automation, and the platform has stayed fast, reliable, and easy to use.

The result, in practice, is fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox — requests that used to disappear for days now move visibly from one owner to the next.

Orbit's dashboard shows every request that's currently in flight, at a glance. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what and when. And the audit log gives compliance teams proof that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
