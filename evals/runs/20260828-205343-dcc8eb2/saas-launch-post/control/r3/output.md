Orbit ships with three things our team focused on:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** every handoff — who owns it, what's blocking it, when it's due — lives on one board

We built an approach that integrates useful features across the stack without piling on complexity, resting on the basics: collaboration and craftsmanship. It's lightweight but still gives teams what they actually need.

The workflow is straightforward: request → review → approve → done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took the product from a scrappy newcomer to one of the more established names in workflow automation. The platform is fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

The result is fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox, and fewer requests that quietly die because nobody knew whose turn it was.

Orbit's dashboard functions as a single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed serves as a running record of who touched what and when. The audit log stands as proof, for compliance teams, that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
