## What's Inside

Orbit ships with three pillars our team obsessed over:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- Visibility: every handoff shows who owns it, what's blocking it, and when it's due, all on one board
- Control: admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them automatically

Orbit ties speed, visibility, and control together in one system, so features across the stack work with each other instead of around each other. It's simple to set up and still covers what teams actually need.

The workflow is straightforward: request -> review -> approve -> done. Over three years, Fluxframe built its way to this position in the automation market. It's fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

The result: fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox, and no request that falls through the cracks. Most teams don't realize how much time this gets back for their managers until they see it for themselves.

Orbit's dashboard is a single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed is a running record of who touched what and when. The audit log is proof, for compliance teams, that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
