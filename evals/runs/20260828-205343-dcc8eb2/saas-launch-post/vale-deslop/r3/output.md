## What's Inside

Orbit ships with three pillars our team obsessed over:

- Speed: approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- Visibility: every handoff (who owns it, what's blocking it, when it's due) lives on one board
- Control: admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them automatically

Orbit integrates these pieces across the stack instead of bolting them together, so teams get what they need without carrying extra overhead.

The workflow is straightforward: request -> review -> approve -> done. Over three years, Fluxframe grew into a leading name in the automation market, and the platform is fast, reliable, and intuitive to match.

The result: fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox, and hours handed back to the managers who used to chase them down.

Orbit's dashboard is a single source of truth for every request in flight. Its activity feed is a running record of who touched what and when, and the audit log is proof, for compliance teams, that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
