## What's Inside

Orbit ships with three pillars our team obsessed over:

- Speed: approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- Visibility: one board shows every handoff, including who owns it, what's blocking it, and when it's due
- Control: admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them automatically after that

We designed Orbit as a new kind of platform, with powerful features that connect across your whole stack. Three things guided the work: excellence, collaboration, and innovation. You get a lightweight tool that still covers what your team needs.

The workflow is simple. Someone sends a request, a reviewer looks at it, they approve it, and it's done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from a weak position in the automation market to a strong one, and then to a dominant one. Orbit is fast and reliable, and people find it intuitive to use.

You'll see fewer stalled approvals waiting in someone's inbox, and your team won't lose requests anymore. Think about why approvals stall at all, and how much time your managers could recover if they didn't. Some teams are surprised by the answer when they finally make the switch.

Orbit's dashboard gives you one source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what, and when. Compliance teams can use the audit log to confirm that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
