## What's Inside

We spent most of our effort on three areas:

- Speed: approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- Visibility: every handoff lives on one board, with its owner, its blocker, and its due date
- Control: admins set the guardrails once and Orbit enforces them automatically

Orbit pulls its features together across the stack, so you aren't stitching tools to each other yourself. We kept it light enough to learn in an afternoon while still covering what an approvals team needs. Collaboration was the design goal from the start.

A request goes to review, gets approved, and it's done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from a weak position in the automation market to a strong one, and then to the lead. Orbit is fast and reliable, and new users find their way around it without a manual.
