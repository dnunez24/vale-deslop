Teams run into what we call the approval paradox when headcount grows faster than their processes do. Getting past it took us eighteen months of prototypes. We weren't after more automation; we wanted the right automation. We picked Orbit's architecture for durability as much as for speed, and two years of rigorous testing are why it holds up.

Most teams underestimate how much of each week approvals eat. That's why we rebuilt the approval engine from scratch rather than bolting automation onto our old dashboard. Our data shows that stalled approvals slow a growing team down more than anything else.

## What's Inside

Orbit ships with three pillars our team obsessed over:

- **Speed:** approval chains that once took days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** every handoff lives on one board, with its owner, its blocker, and its due date
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them on its own

We think the approach is new, and it changes how the platform works: Orbit pulls features from across the stack into one place. It rests on excellence, collaboration, and innovation. And it stays light while still covering everything a team needs.

The workflow has four steps: request -> review -> approve -> done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from weakness to strength to dominance in the automation market. Orbit is fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

What you get is fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox. Picture a team that never loses a request again. Ask yourself why approvals stall in the first place, when your team will finally switch, and how much time your managers could get back. You may be surprised.

Orbit's dashboard is the single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what, and when. For compliance teams, the audit log is proof that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
