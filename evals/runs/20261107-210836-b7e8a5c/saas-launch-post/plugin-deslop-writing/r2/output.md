## What's Inside

We spent most of our effort on three things:

- **Speed:** approval chains that once took days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** one board shows every handoff, who owns it, what's blocking it, and when it's due
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them on its own

We think the approach is new, and we built Orbit to plug into the rest of your stack without friction. The team behind it cares about doing the work well and doing it together. Orbit stays light, but it covers what a team needs.

The flow has four steps: request -> review -> approve -> done. Three years ago Fluxframe was a weak player in automation; today it dominates the market. Orbit is fast and reliable, and new users find their way around it without training.
