Most teams underestimate how much of their week goes to approvals. So we rebuilt the approval engine from scratch rather than bolt automation onto our old dashboard. Our data shows that stalled approvals slow a growing team down more than anything else.

## What's Inside

We focused on three things while building Orbit:

- **Speed:** approval chains that once took days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** one board shows every handoff, who owns it, what's blocking it, and when it's due
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them on its own

Orbit pulls features from across the stack into one place. We designed it around excellence, collaboration, and innovation, and kept it light while still covering what teams need.

The flow is simple: request -> review -> approve -> done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from weakness to strength to dominance in the automation market. Orbit is fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

That means fewer approvals stuck in someone's inbox. Think about what it would mean for your team to never lose a request, why approvals stall in the first place, and how much time your managers could win back. Then ask yourself when your team plans to switch.

The dashboard is the single source of truth for every open request. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what, and when. For compliance teams, the audit log shows that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
