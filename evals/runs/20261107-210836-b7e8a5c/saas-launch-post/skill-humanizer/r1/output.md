We picked Orbit's architecture for durability as well as speed, and it holds up because of two years of rigorous testing, not luck.

Most teams underestimate how much of their week goes to approvals. So instead of bolting automation onto our old dashboard, we rebuilt the approval engine from scratch. Our data shows that stalled approvals slow a growing team down more than anything else.

## What's inside

Our team spent most of its effort on three areas:

- **Speed:** approval chains that once took days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** each handoff, with its owner, its blocker, and its due date, sits on one board
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them on its own

Orbit brings these features together across the stack in one platform. We designed it around collaboration and careful engineering. It stays light while covering what teams actually need, and nothing more.

The workflow is simple: request → review → approve → done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from a weak position in the automation market to a strong one, and then to the lead. Orbit is fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

That means fewer approvals stuck in someone's inbox, and a team that stops losing requests. If you've wondered why approvals stall in the first place, or how much time your managers could get back, the dashboard will show you.

Orbit's dashboard is the single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what and when. For compliance teams, the audit log is proof that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
