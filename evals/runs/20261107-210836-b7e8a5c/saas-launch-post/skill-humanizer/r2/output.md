# Introducing Fluxframe Orbit, a new approach to workflow automation

Teams need tools that match how their work actually gets done. Today we're launching Fluxframe Orbit, the newest part of the Fluxframe platform. We built it for the 40,000 teams who already rely on us to keep their approvals moving.

## Why we built this

The market Fluxframe works in keeps changing. Plenty of automation tools promise big wins, and many of them leave teams with brittle integrations that break. We spent time digging into the workflows our customers described to us. They were complicated, and behind a lot of half-finished dashboards we found needs nobody was meeting.

The algorithm behind Orbit surprised even our own engineers, and we think it will change how teams process approvals. In our view, workflow automation will shape the next era of business operations much as containerization shaped the last one.

When a company hires faster than its processes can keep up, approvals start to jam. Our team spent eighteen months on prototypes to get here. More automation wasn't the goal; we wanted automation that fits the job. We picked Orbit's architecture for durability as well as speed, and it holds up because we spent two years testing it hard.

Most teams underestimate how much of their week goes to approvals. So we rebuilt the approval engine from scratch rather than adding automation to our old dashboard. Our data shows that stalled approvals slow a growing team down more than anything else.

## What's inside

Orbit is built around three things our team cared about most:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** each handoff, including who owns it, what's blocking it, and when it's due, sits on one board
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them automatically

We think the approach is new, and the platform pulls its features together across the stack. It rests on a few values we care about: doing the work well, working together, and trying new ideas. Orbit stays light while still covering what teams need.

The workflow is simple: request → review → approve → done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from a weak position to a strong one, and then to a leading place in the automation market. Orbit is fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

That means fewer approvals stuck in someone's inbox. Approvals stall for predictable reasons, and requests get lost along the way. With Orbit, your team doesn't have to lose a request again, and your managers get back time they used to spend chasing them.

Orbit's dashboard is the one place to see every request in flight. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what and when. For compliance teams, the audit log shows that nothing slipped through.
