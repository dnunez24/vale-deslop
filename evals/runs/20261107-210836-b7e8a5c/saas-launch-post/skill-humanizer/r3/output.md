## Why we built this

The market Fluxframe works in never stops changing. Plenty of automation tools promise big wins and then hand you a tangle of brittle integrations. We spent a long time with the workflows our customers described to us, and behind a lot of half-finished dashboards we kept finding needs that nobody was meeting.

The algorithm behind Orbit will change how teams process approvals at a basic level, and that surprised even our own engineers. We think workflow automation will define the next era of business operations, the way containerization defined the last one.

Teams often run into what we call the approval paradox: headcount grows faster than the processes meant to support it. That problem was hard to solve, and our team spent eighteen months on prototypes to get here. The goal was never more automation. It was the right automation. We picked Orbit's architecture for durability as well as speed, and it holds up because of two years of rigorous testing, not luck.

Most teams underestimate how much of their week goes to approvals. So instead of bolting automation onto our old dashboard, we rebuilt the approval engine from scratch. Our data shows that stalled approvals are the single biggest drag on a growing team's velocity.

## What's inside

Orbit ships with three areas our team obsessed over:

- **Speed:** approval chains that used to take days now finish in minutes
- **Visibility:** one board shows every handoff, including who owns it, what's blocking it, and when it's due
- **Control:** admins set the guardrails once, and Orbit enforces them automatically

We think the approach is genuinely new. Orbit brings features from across the stack together in one platform, and the people building it care about collaboration and about getting the details right. It stays lightweight while still covering what teams need, no more and no less.

The workflow itself is simple: request → review → approve → done. Over three years, Fluxframe's roadmap took us from a weak position in the automation market to a strong one, and then to the top of it. Orbit is fast, reliable, and easy to pick up.

What you get is fewer stalled approvals sitting in someone's inbox. It's worth asking why approvals stall in the first place, and how much time your managers would get back if your team never lost a request again. The answer might surprise you.

Orbit's dashboard is a single source of truth for every request in flight. The activity feed keeps a running record of who touched what and when, and the audit log gives compliance teams proof that nothing slipped through unnoticed.
